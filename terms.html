Terms of Use

By creating a SIDLNE account you agree to these terms. SIDLNE is a free, private calendar for families and the carpool partners they invite.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content

Family chat and other shared notes are for coordinating schedules and rides. You may not post content that is abusive, harassing, hateful, threatening, sexual, or otherwise objectionable, and you may not use SIDLNE to bully or abuse anyone. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report: in Chat, tap ⋯ on any message and choose Report message. The message is hidden for you right away and the SIDLNE team reviews every report within 24 hours.
  • Block: tap ⋯ on a message and choose Block. You won’t see that person’s messages. Unblock any time under Family → Blocked.
  • Remove: family owners and admins can delete any message and remove members under Family → Members.

When a report is confirmed we remove the content and, where needed, remove the person who posted it from SIDLNE.

Your account

Keep your password private. You can delete your account and personal data at any time under Family → Delete my account. See our Privacy Policy.

Contact

Email support@sidlne.app to report a problem or a person.
