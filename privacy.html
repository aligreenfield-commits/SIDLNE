Privacy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

SIDLNE is a shared calendar for families. This page explains what we store, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name and a securely hashed password.
  • Family content you add: family names, athlete first names, teams, events, locations, ride plans and chat messages.
  • Team calendar links you paste in, which we fetch to import schedules.

How it is used

Only to run the app: showing your schedule, syncing team calendars, and sharing your family’s calendar with the people you invite. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Who can see it

Your family’s content is visible only to members of that family. Access rules are enforced in the database itself. Anyone holding your family’s invite code can join, so share it only with people you trust; admins can reset the code and remove members at any time.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, and encrypted in transit. The app keeps a small amount of information in your browser (your sign-in session and preferences) so you stay signed in.

Deleting your data

Go to Family → Delete my account to permanently delete your account. Families you own are deleted along with their events, teams and messages. Leaving a family removes your access but keeps the family’s shared calendar for its other members.

Children

SIDLNE accounts are for parents and guardians aged 18 or older. Children are listed by first name only and do not have accounts.
