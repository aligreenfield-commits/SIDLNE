Install SIDLNE on your phone

SIDLNE works like an app once it’s on your Home Screen: full screen, one tap away, and you stay signed in.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open SIDLNE in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open SIDLNE in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
